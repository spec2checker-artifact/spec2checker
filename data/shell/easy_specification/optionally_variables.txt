**Specification 36: Optionally quote shell-internal integer special variables**
Description: Optionally quote shell-internal, readonly special variables that are integers: `$?`, `$#`, `$$`, `$!`. Prefer quoting named internal integer variables (e.g., `PPID`) for consistency.
Example: `echo "ppid=${PPID}"` (quoted).
Additional Notes: It’s ok to not quote inside `(( ))`.
