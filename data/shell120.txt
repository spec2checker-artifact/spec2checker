Additional Notes: None.

**Specification 118: Avoid `(( ... ))` on same line as other commands without proper spacing**
Description: Put a space after `((` and before `))` for readability.
Example: `(( i += 3 ))` not `((i+=3))`.
Additional Notes: None.

**Specification 119: Use `$(( ... ))` for arithmetic expansion, not backticks with `expr`**
Description: Always use `$(( ... ))` instead of backticks with `expr`.
Example: `addition="$(( X + Y ))"` not `addition="$(expr "${X}" + "${Y}")"`
Additional Notes: None.

**Specification 120: Consistency - when in doubt, be consistent**
Description: Using one style consistently through codebase lets us focus on other issues. Consistency should not justify old style without considering benefits of new style.
Example: Follow local style, but new code may adopt modern practices.
Additional Notes: Limits to consistency.
